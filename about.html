Meet Dorica & Philimon

Dorphil Restaurant is a warm, family-run dining destination built by Dorica and Philimon. Their vision was to create a welcoming place where locals, travelers, and safari guests could enjoy freshly prepared meals in Mfuwe.

The restaurant brings together community spirit, friendly service, and food that feels familiar, satisfying, and proudly local.
